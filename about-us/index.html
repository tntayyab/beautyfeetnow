About Beauty Feet Now

We believe that healthy, pain-free feet are the essential foundation of total body mobility, posture, and self-confidence.

Founded to bring clarity to an overwhelming marketplace of gimmicky pedicure gadgets and unverified claims, Beauty Feet Now is an independent digital testing publication dedicated exclusively to lower extremity health, dermatology-backed foot care, and therapeutic wellness tools.

Our Mission

Millions of people needlessly suffer from painful cracked heels, painful plantar fasciitis, and thick friction calluses simply because generic beauty advice fails to address the unique physiology of foot skin. The skin on the soles of our feet is up to 20 times thicker than facial skin and lacks sebaceous oil glands, requiring specialized keratolytic formulations and precision tools.

Our goal is to cut through commercial marketing noise, test products objectively, and provide actionable guides that deliver visible, long-lasting relief.

Our Editorial Standards & E-E-A-T Commitment

  • Hands-On Bench Testing: We evaluate electric foot files, creams, and massagers in real-world scenarios—measuring actual motor stalling thresholds, battery runtimes, and skin absorption rates.
  • Podiatrist & Dermatology Alignment: All guidance is cross-referenced with established clinical podiatry practices. We prioritize safe treatment protocols, especially for diabetics and those with compromised circulation.
  • Zero Brand Sponsorships: We purchase or independently source products. Manufacturers never dictate our scores, review verdicts, or ranking orders.

Meet the Team

Our lead editor, Elena Rostova, specializes in dermatological skincare formulations and biomechanical wellness aids. With over 8 years of product testing experience, Elena leads our laboratory comparisons and ensures every recommendation adheres to strict clinical safety criteria.
