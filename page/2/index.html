Frequently Asked Foot Care Questions

Are electric callus removers safe to use on dry cracked heels?

Yes, when used with proper technique. Start with a fine or medium roller head on clean, dry feet. Never press too hard into deep cracks or fissures to prevent skin tears. Follow up immediately with a hydrating 20% to 40% urea cream to lock in moisture.

What percentage of Urea is best for severe foot calluses?

Dermatologists and podiatrists typically recommend 40% Urea cream for thick calluses and severely cracked heels. Urea at this concentration acts as a potent keratolytic agent that dissolves dead keratin bonds without stinging.

How often should I use a heated foot massager for plantar fasciitis?

For acute morning arch pain and plantar fasciitis flare-ups, a 15 to 20-minute session once or twice daily is optimal. Use gentle kneading pressure and mild heat to stimulate blood circulation and loosen the plantar fascia ligament.
