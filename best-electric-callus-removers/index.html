How to choose an electric callus remover

Before selecting a tool, consider how severe your calluses are and where you prefer to do your foot care:

  • Motor Torque vs. Stalling: Cheaper AA battery files often grind to a dead halt the moment they encounter tough skin. Choose high-torque lithium-ion models like the Own Harmony or PRITECH.
  • Wet vs. Dry Use: If you love doing pedicures directly in the bath or shower, look for true IPX7 ratings like the Amope Pedi Perfect Wet & Dry. Never submerge devices that only advertise water-resistant heads.
  • Roller Grits: Having multiple coarseness grades allows you to aggressively take down the thickest calluses first, then polish with a fine head to prevent rough snags on socks.
