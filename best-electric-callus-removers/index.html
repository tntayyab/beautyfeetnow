Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How often should I use these foot care products?

For electronic callus removers, treatments should be limited to once every 7 to 10 days on 100% dry skin. For cracked heel creams with 40% urea, apply daily at bedtime under cotton socks until fissures close, then transition to 20% urea for weekly maintenance.

Are these products safe for people with diabetes or neuropathy?

Diabetic patients must avoid aggressive rasps or high-abrasion electric grinders without direct podiatric supervision. We recommend gentle nano-glass files, 20% urea creams, and physician-approved orthotics with low-friction top covers.
