Editorial Guidelines & Medical Review Standards

At Beauty Feet Now, our mission is to empower individuals with evidence-based, medically verified, and clinically tested guidance on lower extremity health, biomechanics, and therapeutic foot care.

Because foot health deeply impacts physical mobility, chronic pain levels, postural alignment, and overall systemic well-being—particularly for populations managing diabetes or peripheral neuropathy—we treat foot care information with the highest standard of editorial integrity and scientific rigor under Google's Your Money or Your Life (YMYL) standards.

1. The Four-Tier Medical Review Workflow

Every clinical protocol, product comparison, and educational article published on Beauty Feet Now undergoes a strict, multi-stage editorial and clinical review before going live:

  1. Primary Research & Clinical Synthesis: Our technical medical writers examine peer-reviewed studies from authoritative dermatological and orthopedic databases, evaluating physiological mechanisms, biomechanical forces, and safety boundaries.
  2. Physical Product Bench Testing: When reviewing appliances (such as electric callus removers, heated foot massagers, or orthotic insoles), tools are tested against objective engineering benchmarks including motor stall torque, thermal rise, vibration amplitude, and shore durometer rigidity.
  3. Board-Certified Medical Review: Articles are thoroughly audited by licensed podiatrists (Doctor of Podiatric Medicine, DPM) or board-certified dermatologists. Reviewers verify anatomical accuracy, clinical dosing (such as urea concentrations), contraindications, and diabetic safety precautions.
  4. Periodic Scheduled Re-Auditing: Medical literature evolves continuously. We conduct quarterly audits of all clinical protocols to ensure recommendations align with the latest clinical practice guidelines issued by the American Podiatric Medical Association (APMA) and American College of Foot and Ankle Surgeons (ACFAS).

2. Clinical Evidence & Sourcing Standards

We refuse to cite unverified blogs, manufacturer press releases, sponsored influencer posts, or anecdotal claims. Our primary citations originate exclusively from:

  • Peer-Reviewed Medical Journals: Publications indexed in PubMed/MEDLINE, including the Journal of the American Podiatric Medical Association (JAPMA), Dermatologic Surgery, The Foot, and Gait & Posture.
  • Authoritative Medical Institutions: Clinical consensus statements from the Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, American Academy of Dermatology (AAD), and Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).
  • Systematic Reviews: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews assessing randomized controlled trials (RCTs).

Every article features a transparent, numbered bibliography linking directly to source abstracts or full-text clinical publications.

3. Commercial Independence & Affiliate Separation

To preserve absolute objectivity, Beauty Feet Now maintains a strict barrier between our editorial team and our business operations:

  • Editorial Autonomy: Reviewers, editors, and contributing podiatrists have zero financial incentives tied to specific brands or product rankings.
  • No Paid Placements: We do not accept paid reviews, sponsored ranking spots, or gifted merchandise in exchange for favorable coverage. Products evaluated in our roundups are independently purchased or evaluated strictly according to documented laboratory protocols.
  • Affiliate Transparency: While Beauty Feet Now participates in affiliate programs (including the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program) to fund our independent laboratory testing, affiliate links are marked with rel="nofollow sponsored" and have zero influence on editorial scoring.

4. Corrections & Continuous Improvement Policy

Accuracy is our highest priority. If an error of fact, outdated clinical citation, or incorrect product specification is identified by our editors or brought to our attention by readers, we act swiftly:

  • Expedited Correction: Factual discrepancies are reviewed by our lead medical editor within 24 business hours.
  • Transparent Revision: Material updates to treatment protocols or safety warnings are documented with an updated timestamp and revision note at the top of the article.
  • Submitting Corrections: Readers, clinicians, and researchers are encouraged to report any potential inaccuracies directly to our editorial desk via email at support@beautyfeetnow.com.

Medical Disclaimer: The information provided on Beauty Feet Now is for educational and informational purposes only and does not substitute for professional medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or individualized treatment. Always consult a board-certified podiatrist (DPM), dermatologist, or qualified healthcare provider before initiating any foot care protocol or using high-concentration keratolytic formulations.
